Frittata

Description

This single serving frittata is a quick-and-easy way to style up breakfast or brunch. In a single serving, you can use small bits of leftovers like potatoes, asparagus, bacon, mushrooms, or spinach, or make this just as the recipe calls for.

Ingredients

  • 1 large egg
  • 3 large egg whites
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 slice bacon
  • 1/2 bell pepper, any color, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons chopped red onion
  • 3 tablespoons chopped mushrooms
  • 1 small tomato, sliced
  • 1/3 cup shredded shar Cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  2. Whisk whole egg and egg whites together in a small bowl. Season with salt and pepper, and set aside.
  3. Heat an 8-inch oven-proof skillet over medium heat, and add bacon. Cook until crisp, remove to a paper towel to drain, and crumble. Add bell pepper, onion, and mushrooms to the same skillet, and saute for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Scatter bacon evenly over veggies. Pour egg mixture into skillet, covering veggies. Top with sliced tomatoes, and sprinkle with cheese.
  5. Transfer skillet to the preheated oven; bake until eggs are just set, about 7 minutes. Serve whole or cut into wedges and serve immediately.
